`NeighborRequest` gains `shell` (`NeighborShell`: `half` or `full`), `binEdge`, `origin` and `excludeSelfImages` at ordinals 12 to 15, after `systems`. The fields before them keep their ordinals and wire types. The values of the `UInt8` fields `format` and `cut` are named by the constants `neighborFormatPairs`, `neighborFormatCluster4`, `neighborCutStrict` and `neighborCutLoose`, with the meanings of `rgpot_nlist_request.shape` and `.cut`; `mode` is documented as reserved, with 0 refused. The potentials schema protocol minor is 4.
